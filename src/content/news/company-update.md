For further information regarding the research efforts of the company, please contact the company. Through our research, we aspire to make a meaningful impact on neurodegenerative disease, and we believe it is possible to not only slow the disease progression but halt progression altogether. Our first interest is in MND/ALS but we have a burgeoning interest in Parkinson's Disease and Alzheimer's Disease. Research in the area of neurodegeneration has advanced considerably over the past 5 years and we now have a much better idea of the processes that drive the disease. We welcome media enquiries.
